# SubLoop

**Pay for one streaming service. Not eight.**

SubLoop rotates your streaming subscriptions so you always have something to watch — without paying for everything at once.

## The problem

You're subscribed to Netflix, Disney+, Max, Prime Video, Spotify, and a handful of others. You watch a few hours a week, but you pay for all of them, every single month.

## The idea

Keep one service active, pause the rest, and rotate. Every time you come back to a service, it's stacked with new seasons and movies you haven't seen. Same content, a fraction of the bill.

## What SubLoop does

- Track your subscriptions: price, billing date, and priority.
- Set your monthly budget.
- Keep a watchlist for each service.
- Get a monthly rotation plan that maximizes what you watch for what you pay.
- See exactly how much you save versus keeping everything active.

## The payoff

| Scenario | Monthly cost |
| --- | ---: |
| All services active | $1,237 |
| SubLoop optimized plan | $419 |
| **Estimated yearly savings** | **$9,816** |
